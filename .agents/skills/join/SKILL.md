# Join Wildfire (Codex)

1. Read `CLAUDE.md` (the agent's standing rules; it starts with `AGENTS.md`) and `board.json`.
2. Follow `.claude/commands/join.md` step by step. The student's name is the text after `$join`: use it

Codex notes are at the top of `AGENTS.md` ("Playing with Codex").
