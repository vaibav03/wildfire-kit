# Play Wildfire (Codex)

1. Read `CLAUDE.md` (the agent's standing rules; it starts with `AGENTS.md`), `board.json` and `player.json`.
2. Follow `.claude/commands/play.md` step by step, turn after turn. Where it says `/join`, follow
   `.claude/commands/join.md` (the student types `$join Your Name`); where it says `/play`, `$play`.
3. Keep playing on your own: don't end your turn to report progress. Stop only for the one YES/NO
   question, when your team is OUT, at GAME OVER, or at the call budget.

Codex notes are at the top of `AGENTS.md` ("Playing with Codex").
